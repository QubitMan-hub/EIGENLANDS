COZY FARM – free blocky 3D asset pack
Made with BlockImagine · https://blockimagine.com

30 low-poly, blocky models with pixel-art textures, 25 of them rigged and animated:
10 characters, 7 animals and 13 props in one scale and one palette.

FOLDERS
  glb/         Ready-to-use models with embedded texture and animation (glTF binary).
               Works in Godot 4, Unity 6 (glTFast), Blender and three.js.
  blockbench/  Editable Blockbench source files (.bbmodel), open in Blockbench (free, blockbench.net).
  textures/    The pixel-art texture of each model (PNG).
  previews/    A preview image of each model.

TIPS
  - Keep pixel textures crisp: set texture filtering to "Nearest" (Godot: Filter Nearest; Unity: Point).
  - All models share one scale (1 unit = 1 m; the farmer is about 2 m tall), so they fit together out of the box.
  - Loops are seamless; play the clip on repeat. The barn has one clip per door: play both together.

  sentences into blocky, rigged and animated 3D models, and then reviewed and touched up by a human.
  Want a model that isn't in here? Describe it at https://blockimagine.com – new accounts get free credits.
