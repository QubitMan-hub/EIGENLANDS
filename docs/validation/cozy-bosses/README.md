# Cozy boss presentation and combat

All 14 existing boss identities remain. The importer now samples them on a shared two-pixel cluster grid at the same world size. This reduces fine texture density without runtime blur, new collisions, anatomy changes or another generic recolor. Original sources remain intact. The 28 production day/night PNGs are 50.7% smaller in total (14,694,307 → 7,237,790 bytes); decoded texture dimensions and GPU memory budget are unchanged. See [export sizes](export-size.json). Four authored facings, frame ordering, ground pivots, binary alpha, portraits and day/night variants remain compatible.

Pursuit now has bounded acceleration, including cached route corners, instead of instant full-speed direction changes. Casts and charges keep their precise timing; windups stop locomotion. Walk cadence is distance-driven. Ordinary hit flashes no longer replace committed boss poses; real poise breaks still show hurt and interrupt attacks. Fast windup vibration is removed, white hit flashes are restrained, and simultaneous ground hits coalesce their sound/shake while retaining every visible impact. Small-radius boss strikes now trigger the same visible attack release as larger strikes.

Every boss alternates its existing signature with a distinct additional pattern: crown splash, rootbreak, fractured crossing, quiet bell, conductor's fork, prismatic cage, ashen jaws, winter's compass, undertow wake, first crown, remembering wake, unwritten sentence, last hammer, and six-gate convergence. Geometry is fixed when warned, uses gaps or changing lanes, and leaves recovery windows. Difficulty comes from positioning and phase changes; health was not inflated. Design principles draw on Zelda's readable openings, Hades' differentiated ground patterns and Monster Hunter's committed windups/recovery, adapted to the existing game.

Concrete fixes: Colossus's expanding impacts stay at their warned origin; special casts cannot interrupt normal committed attacks or overlap pending spells; a scripted windup cannot fall through into an extra generic melee hit. All bosses cancel queued casts on poise break, require a visible live target, and clean up after 60 seconds without nearby living players. Ancient casts remain locked through their final impact and recovery. Packs are capped at six, summoned adds grant no ordinary rewards and are removed with their boss. Existing ancient contribution and trophy rules remain.
