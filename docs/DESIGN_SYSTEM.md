# EIGENLANDS design system

Authoritative existing art details: [art style guide](art/style-guide.md). Requested direction: [master request](design/master-request.md). Experience: medieval field journal and crafted adventurer equipment, with restrained quantum cyan/violet reserved for magic. The world is the primary surface; compact functional chrome must leave movement and telegraphs visible.

Geometry: 32×16 2:1 diamonds, existing isokit/Iso geometry, projected visible faces, correct ground/hand anchors, integer device-pixel scaling, nearest-neighbour textures. People 24×32 and eight facings; held objects use personGrip. Creatures retain their directional animation interfaces. Use original or verified licensed art.

UI direction: warm parchment text, ink/navy recesses, weathered bronze and wood framing, square pixel-stepped corners and selective rune decoration. Health carmine, magic mineral teal, stamina ochre, food leaf green, water blue; always accompany colour with labels/icons. Quantum accents are functional state, not generic decoration. Pixel display face for titles/short labels; readable body text for dialogue, recipes and descriptions. Font assets remain local.

Spacing: 4px base, 8px related controls, 12px panel gutters, 16px sections. Keyboard focus must remain visible. Touch controls at least 44 CSS pixels, safe-area aware. Mobile landscape is preferred, with explicit portrait fallback and accessible scrolling. Windows have consistent title/back/close controls and retain focus/input protections.

Motion target: damage trails communicate delayed health loss (existing boss ghost fill; player damage trail remains open); resource changes ease over short durations. No perpetual decoration over the playfield. Respect reduced motion, suppress shake and freeze nonessential loops. Preserve combat telegraphs and cues.

Maps: generate/cache explored chunks only, never the infinite world. World map and minimap share data, markers, legend and waypoints. Never display undiscovered content as explored. Inventory retains real item identity, comparison, categories, filtering and controller/touch affordances.

Anti-patterns: SaaS cards, pill buttons, random purple/blue gradients, glass panels, oversized HUDs, duplicate icons, blurry sprite rotations, bright texture noise, unreachable decoration, hidden collision, invented statistics or placeholder mechanics described as complete.

Visual acceptance: inspect a batched desktop/mobile set of real gameplay, low health, inventory/crafting, progression, map, combat/boss and settings states; one consolidated fix pass and at most one confirmation pass. Record exact tested surfaces in CONTINUE_HERE.md. Screens not inspected remain unverified.

Implemented UI components this pass: compact bronze-bound player ledger with current/max health and numeric progressbar accessibility; matte green-ink windows with parchment labels; explicit quick-bar/backpack sections, named search/category/order controls, selectable item details/actions and keyboard equipment buttons. Filtering and sorting operate only on view indices. Windows use labelled dialogs, visible focus, a Tab loop and focus return. Opt-in hunger/thirst now use authoritative private meters; stamina and temperature remain design targets until their rules exist. Existing class resource bars remain authoritative.


Atlas/minimap implementation: both use the same journal of visited tile IDs, rendered in the actual iso projection through cached 128×64 chunk charts. Unknown terrain is opaque ink; trees, rock, lamps, flowers, water, paving and floors use quiet material colours. Player facing uses the same world projection; travellers are cyan circles, villagers warm squares, enemies rust triangles and waypoints rose squares. Enemy display defaults off. Ordinary location markers require discovery, and completed shrine markers are suppressed. Explicitly tracked quest-described homes and the known starting village may be pinned over fog without charting terrain; unseen live NPC positions remain hidden. Full atlas offers native controls for layer/location/filter selection, drag and keyboard/button panning, zoom, centering and named waypoints. Compact landscape tools scroll horizontally, the map keeps protected focus, and voice chrome yields to dialogs. M opens/closes the atlas; remapped labels reflect actual keys.

Persistence/limits: chart, waypoints, filters, size and visibility are local to account + seed on this browser. IndexedDB archives explored cells beyond the 512-page resident cache; a compact discovery index keeps previously discovered destinations available. Terrain reads query viewport bounds and load at most 64 pages per batch, prioritizing the nearest 384 at extreme zoom-out. Raster cache stays at 512 pages (~16 MiB at full occupancy). A bounded localStorage snapshot and pending-cell recovery journal protect recent progress during interrupted writes. Storage status and loading feedback belong inside the atlas, never a new HUD panel. Browser quotas/cleared data remain limits; prolonged write failure can lose older unsaved terrain after 512 pending pages and must be reported. Cross-device sync, private-world keys and unimplemented kingdom/realm maps remain future work.

Quiet gameplay chrome: persistent farming/tutorial directions and quest objectives belong in Journal, never a second playfield tracker. Journal is directly reachable; utility actions live in a native popover Menu with outside/Escape dismissal and labelled 44px controls. Time/day/season and town direction use a compact strip; the atlas retains its visibility/size preferences. Idle chat starts folded unless the user saved otherwise, and only two recent notifications are visible. Touch layout keeps travel status, minimap, chat opener and thumb zones apart. Transient combat and nearby interaction cues remain visible.

Equipment detail uses a semantic before/after/change table only when passive stats change, including lost/gained set bonuses. Signed numbers supplement colour. Rings expose the exact replacement slot; preview and Equip share it. Totals retain progression bonuses and use the shared armour/Focus data and crafted ring values. Plain swords stay in the quick-bar; the preview does not invent DPS or alter server rules.

Encounter presentation: one compact header takes priority during a boss encounter, with actual numeric health and shared phase thresholds. Boss start/phase prose belongs inside that header. Ordinary selected targets expose real poise, disposition/modifiers and combat state; they do not invent levels from health. Damage trails snap on a new identity or healing. Travel text yields during encounters; small landscape phones also yield the minimap. Combat chrome yields to dialogs and defeat screens. Retain high contrast and reduced motion.

Journal Journey reads current level/XP and Circuit progression, shared unlock milestones and existing summoning requirements. Its visible page refreshes from the existing frame without polling timers or new persistence. Choosing a path still uses the existing Circuit Board. Focusable Journal pages receive native reading/scroll keys and remain inside dialog focus protection. Do not show boss victory checkmarks without authoritative history.

Atlas destinations: collapsible Quest and Party directories live inside the full atlas, initially closed. Quest triangles use warm gold; party circles use muted lilac and appear only on the full map. Nearby travellers keep their existing discovery rules. Quest locations require charted ground: current synchronized NPCs take precedence over clearly labelled known homes; read objectives use discovered landmarks. Gather/craft/kill objectives show Journal guidance without fabricated locations. Inventory determines delivery readiness; finished objectives disappear and ready quests switch to the giver. Authorized party positions are labelled last reported, including distant shards and coordinate layers, and can appear over opaque fog without revealing terrain. Offline/unknown positions cannot be centered. Party snapshots stay in memory; marker preferences are saved. The existing frame updates the open map, preserving focused controls during ordinary position updates. Full instructions and progress counts remain in the Journal. Native Space/Enter activate the disclosures without gameplay input, and summaries participate in the modal focus loop. Mobile landscape stacks the directories; closed sections do not stretch beside an open section. No added playfield panels.

Circuit workshop: the Board inherits the bronze/ink-green/parchment material palette; bright phase colours belong to crystals, rails and actual outcomes. Guides lives inside the modal and has a compact pattern directory, native orb selection, semantic current/pattern comparison and explicit draft application. Nothing auto-attunes. Class milestones use shared thresholds and detail expands on demand. Preserve numeric odds, slip disclosure, stored/pending Lock-ins, keyboard focus, expert science and discovered codex content. Do not claim new unlocks or challenge rewards for trying a local pattern.

Welcoming-world guidance: First steps to Hadamere stays inside Journal Quests/How to…, with a collapsed checklist and actual action progress. Controls lists all current bindings; Q/E/R/F and X interaction are the new defaults, preserving customized controls. One selected quest reuses the travel strip and a gold triangle, never a second objective panel. Direction uses the same isometric screen projection as movement. Sanctuary is a text state in the strip, not a new effect or overlay.

Lumen story pages render inside the Circuit Board. Outside it, the sprite may remain in the world but the floating text panel stays hidden and cannot consume gameplay input.

Weapon field guide: a semantic before/after table appears only for a selected weapon in Pack, using the existing bronze/ink/parchment surface. Neutral numbers distinguish damage from healing and per-bolt output from counts; never imply a total DPS upgrade. Extra timing/effects stay in a native disclosure. Shared level requirements explain disabled equipment actions, and the open pack refreshes on real level changes. No gameplay chrome is added.

Travel needs: Cozy hides food/water meters; Adventure adds two labelled native progress bars inside the existing ledger, with numeric values and low-state colour. The Journal owns preference/rates/remedies. No screen-wide warning or floating survival panel. Voice/toast positioning follows the actual ledger height through native resize observation, including names and buffs; do not hard-code a second HUD height.

Companions & Echoes owns all wildlife-bond instructions, nearby forage offers and trait details. Use bronze/ink/parchment, natural critter portraits, native Wildlife bonds disclosure, 44px controls, numeric active slots and preserved keyboard focus/reading position on updates. Keep summon/dismiss distinct from permanent release. Living bond markers/health are quiet warm tones; violet remains the echo identity. No extra permanent HUD.

Movement personalization belongs in Settings → Controls: a labelled native keyboard/mouse select with 44px height, concise held-RMB/release and key-then-click guidance. No new permanent playfield panel. Directional aim uses the existing iso dashed line/ring and a static pressed highlight on its ability button; it yields to gameplay-blocking UI and respects reduced motion. Preview distance is the maximum possible form reach before a quantum outcome, not guaranteed damage.

Control overlay priority: voice chrome yields to Settings; explanatory combat hints yield while a directional skill is armed so its projected aim lane remains visible. Telegraphs, selected-target health and direct combat feedback remain.

Melee weapon patterns live at the existing Anvil & Forge: a native, initially expanded Weapon patterns disclosure contains the existing recipe list. Interference forging remains in a separate native disclosure with its original material/phase/rhythm controls. Disclosure headers and craft buttons have 44px targets; pattern rows use ink/bronze ledger materials. No new HUD or permanent weapon panel. Pack comparisons report the actual family damage, reach, recovery, poise and arc, with timing/effects still disclosed. Level restrictions use the current equipment action/lock text. Preserve the ordinary sword quick-bar bonus and distinguish it from the equipped main hand.
