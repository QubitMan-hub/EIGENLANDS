# Living guilds and homesteads

Implement inline; no subagents. Preserve the crafting catalog, authored art, world revisions, controls and saves.

1. Procedural guild commissions: deterministic rotating escort/rescue/investigation/diplomacy journeys, server-owned route progression, physical people/clues, branching choices, deadlines and persisted recovery. Extend the existing quest ledger, not a duplicate reward system.
2. Party kill credit: use existing party membership and nearby living players; never duplicate loot or grant remote credit. Add dedicated guild staff through the NPC renderer and original hall fittings.
3. Settlement management: validate owned enclosed housing and furniture, charge bounded upkeep, assign residents, track happiness and unlock services/charter tiers through the owned guild board. Revalidate homes on edits, persist state and protect ownership.
4. Livestock: bounded collision-aware yard roaming and trained horse/Glimmerhart riding, preserving the shelter economy, reduced motion and player prediction. No free teleport, orphaned mount or stale ownership after handoffs.
5. Validate hostile input, save/reconnect, destroyed homes, blocked paths, expiry, full inventory and party range with units/server/browser checks. Keep remaining scope honest; physical-device testing remains unavailable.
