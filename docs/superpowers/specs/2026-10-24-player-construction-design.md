# Player construction design

Implement the user's one-go request inline, without subagents or another approval cycle. Preserve current houses, farms, inventory, quantum gameplay, solo saves and multiplayer protocol compatibility.

Inspiration reviewed before implementation: Minecraft's modular resource-to-piece building loop (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minecraft), Terraria's build/explore sandbox (https://store.steampowered.com/app/105600/Terraria/), and functional carpentry/access in Project Zomboid (https://pzwiki.net/wiki/Building). Official Minecraft article guesses returned 404 and wiki.gg returned 403; no artwork is copied or imported.

Use craftable timber/stone floors and walls, timber doors, roof pieces and stairs. Arbitrary floor plans, not prebuilt house templates. One structural cell can contain a floor, wall/door and roof while ordinary furniture/stations/chests remain independent. Materials stay native 32×16 projected diamonds, with cached geometry from isokit and current palette. Roof cutaways expose furnished interiors. Main-world houses have ground and upper floors, using the existing separate upper-coordinate architecture rather than a new physics engine. The upper reserved band expands to accommodate construction across the main-world play region; outside it, stairs must refuse safely rather than landing in generated terrain.

Persist structural cells in their own table/local save namespace and replicate through existing chunk/OBJ transport. The structural record is the source of truth for terrain/collision; no separately persisted tile diff can diverge from ownership. Keep existing object rows and save formats intact. A migration only adds a table. Shared ChunkStore composes structural collision identically for authority and prediction. Owner is assigned from the authenticated account, never input. Foreign edits, edits to authored settlements, player entombment and unsupported upper floors are refused without consuming pieces. Structural ownership also guards ordinary placement/removal on those cells.

Doors toggle collision. Stairs create a paired upper landing, validate reach and landing collision before travel, and share the existing transition/reset behavior. Dismantling returns real items only when inventory can receive them; occupied/furnished/supporting cells cannot be removed. Removal must preserve an exit for occupants upstairs. Rotatable pieces expose a compact conditional native control, not a permanent HUD. Existing interact/touch actions place and use; pickaxe removes owned construction. Journal supplies controls and recipes use current crafting infrastructure.

Tradeoffs: two functional floors use existing coordinate layers; arbitrary vertical skyscrapers and structural-collapse physics are not introduced. Upper floors are solid void outside built floors. No unverified external assets, dependency or competing town renderer. Review data compatibility, chunk boundaries, permissions, refunds, stairs and solo/server parity before integration.
