# Privacy (draft policy for EIGENLANDS)

**What we store:** an account id; for Google sign-in your Google subject id and verified email address (never your password: Google handles authentication); for guests a hash of a random device secret; your character (name, position, level, XP) and the world edits you make.
**What we do not store:** passwords, payment data, precise location, or analytics identifiers beyond what you see above.
**Cookies/local storage:** your session token, device secret, settings and tutorial progress, kept in your browser's local storage.
**Deletion:** Settings → "Delete my account and data" permanently removes your account and character (`DELETE /api/auth/account`). World edits are shared world state and are kept without being linked to you.
**Contact:** add your operator contact before launch. This is a development draft, not legal advice.
