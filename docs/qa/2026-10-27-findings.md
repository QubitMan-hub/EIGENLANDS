## Confirmed AI defects — fixed

- Goblins acquire a never-seen player through a continuous solid wall. Target acquisition tests distance without line of sight.
- Pursuers slide against an intervening wall instead of finding its nearby end; return-to-territory movement has the same direct-vector limitation.
- Enemy circular melee strikes test range and dodge state without checking intervening terrain at impact.

Fix: require sight for acquisition and attack windup; pursue a six-second last-seen position using cached, radius-aware, no-corner-cutting A* after direct movement is blocked. Limit route searches to four per world tick and 256 nodes per search. Check terrain cover again at strike impact. Existing enemy roles and attack participation limits remain intact.

Proof: 18 focused checks across QA balance, QA AI and combat2 pass, including cover appearing after acquisition and a previously visible target around a wall.
