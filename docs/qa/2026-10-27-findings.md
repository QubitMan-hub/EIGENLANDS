## Confirmed AI defects — fixed

- Goblins acquire a never-seen player through a continuous solid wall. Target acquisition tests distance without line of sight.
- Pursuers slide against an intervening wall instead of finding its nearby end; return-to-territory movement has the same direct-vector limitation.
- Enemy circular melee strikes test range and dodge state without checking intervening terrain at impact.

Fix: require sight for acquisition and attack windup; pursue a six-second last-seen position using cached, radius-aware, no-corner-cutting A* after direct movement is blocked. Limit route searches to four per world tick and 256 nodes per search. Check terrain cover again at strike impact. Existing enemy roles and attack participation limits remain intact.

Proof: 18 focused checks across QA balance, QA AI and combat2 pass, including cover appearing after acquisition and a previously visible target around a wall.

## Combat contact timing — fixed

Reproduction: tap attack or release a charged heavy; the immediate damage/projectile event previously started player frame 0 (the anticipation key), so the body visibly wound up after the hit. Player release presentation now starts at the contact key, moves through follow-through and spends most of the remaining time settling. Genuine anticipation is shown while the authoritative/predicted held-charge state is active. Boss attack timelines retain their separate windup/strike mapping; damage, cooldowns and projectile authority are unchanged.

Proof: seven focused combat/equipment checks pass, including the unchanged boss timeline, local prediction, observed shooters and late hit confirmations. `e2e/combat-contact.mjs` passes with real keyboard input: opening strike, held charge, heavy release and painted recovery; no late windup during the release or browser errors. The rebuilt solo bundle passes TypeScript and production build.

## Fishing, combat and transport cohesion

Cast cancellation/expiry now explicitly ends the matching client session, clearing its rod/bobber state instead of waiting for a local timer. Stale bite messages cannot recreate a cancelled cast at (0,0). Combat cooldown, dodge, death, mounting and minecart riding interrupt casts, with a second check at hook receipt to close the between-ticks race. Peaceful boat fishing remains supported; its rod is visible and the line follows the actual rendered hand/transport offset. Six focused authority checks and workspace type checks pass. Boat presentation has type/anchor inspection, but has not yet received a fresh browser playtest.

## Weapon identity and motion

Melee body motion previously remained identical across spears, blades and heavy weapons. Added shared slash/thrust/cleave families, selected from both base weapon IDs and inherited family metadata. Contact/recovery follows the existing attack lifetime; charge uses the matching anticipation. Damage, reach and cooldown balance are unchanged. A held-edge test found greatsword tips clipped in overhead poses; expanded transparent held bounds and corrected the common anchor rather than shrinking the art. NPC art remains the restored design.

Proof: eleven focused action/equipment/combat checks, 1,344 action frames, 3,120 appearance frames and weapon-edge coverage pass. Production bundle and live opening/charge/heavy release checks recorded with this batch. No full multiplayer stress or physical-device run is implied.
